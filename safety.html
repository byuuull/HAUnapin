Angelite safety protocol

Safety First.

Keeping the Holy Angel University community safe and honest while reuniting people with their belongings.

Meeting Safely

Never meet a stranger in a private or secluded place. All hand-overs happen at the designated campus Security Office.

  • The Security Office is the main exchange point.
  • Exchanges happen during official University hours.

Verifying Ownership

Before handing over an item, make sure the claimant is the rightful owner. Ask for details that were not in the public listing.

Pro tips“Can you describe the lock-screen wallpaper?” or “What’s inside the small side pocket?”

Ask to see a valid HAU student or employee ID.

Protecting Privacy

Be careful with what you share in public reports. Leave out anything that could lead to identity theft or harassment.

  • Don’t show full ID numbers or home addresses.
  • Use the claim form or the Security Office to get in touch.

Reporting Suspicious Activity

If a claim looks fraudulent or you feel unsafe during a hand-over, tell campus security and the platform team straight away.

Still have questions about safety?

Our team can walk you through the return process.
