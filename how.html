How It Works.

Getting lost items back at Holy Angel University is built on community trust. Whether you’ve lost something important or found someone else’s, here’s how the reunion works.

The search process

I Lost Something

Follow these three steps to improve your chances of getting your item back.

  1. Create a report

    Describe the item, add a photo, its last known location and anything unique like stickers or scratches.

  2. Pin the location

    Use the campus map to mark where you last had it, so finders in the same building can match it.

  3. Verify & recover

    When a match turns up, request to claim it, then confirm your identity and the item details at the Security Office.

The honest path

I Found Something

Be a campus hero. Every item returned strengthens our community of honesty, and the simple check at the Security Office makes sure found items reach their rightful owners.

Secure surrender

Drop the item off at the University Security Office or the nearest security post.

Quick digital log

Log the item here in under two minutes so the owner can find it on the map.

Reunion success

Once the owner collects it, the report is marked returned and added to our success stories.
