Section one

Privacy Policy

Welcome to HAUnapin Natin ’Yan!. Your privacy matters to us as members of the Holy Angel University community. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it and how we protect it.

1. Data we collect

To run the lost-and-found service, we collect:

  • Account information: name, student or employee number, and HAU email address.
  • Item details: photos, descriptions and times for lost or found items.
  • Location data: the campus spot you pin on the map. We never track your phone’s location.
  • Messages: claim requests and messages you send through the site.

2. How we use your data

  • Matching lost item reports with found item reports.
  • Helping the Security Office verify ownership before a hand-over.
  • Keeping simple campus statistics, like the most active areas.
  • Improving the campus map and search.

3. Data protection

Public reports only show your first name and last initial. Your email is shared only with the Security Office when you claim or report an item. We don’t sell or trade personal information.

Section two

Terms of Service

By using HAUnapin Natin ’Yan!, you agree to these terms, which are here to keep a culture of honesty and care within HAU.

1. User conduct & honesty

Everyone must act with integrity. You must not:

  • Post false or misleading reports about lost or found items.
  • Try to claim items that don’t belong to you.
  • Use the platform for harassment or anything unrelated to lost and found.
  • Share other users’ personal information outside the platform.

2. Limitation of liability

HAUnapin Natin ’Yan! is a student-run platform. We help people connect, but we don’t hold items and can’t guarantee every item will be found or returned. Items are held and released by the University Security Office under HAU’s own rules.

3. Account termination

Accounts that break these terms, such as by making false claims, may be suspended or removed, and the matter may be passed to the Office of Student Services and Affairs.
