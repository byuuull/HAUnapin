Our story & mission

Building a culture of Honesty.

HAUnapin Natin ’Yan! started as a student-led project to solve a common campus struggle: getting lost things back to the people who own them.

Our Mission

To give every Angelite a secure, simple and transparent way to practise integrity. We use the campus map and clear hand-over steps to make recovering lost property easier while building trust across the University.

Our Vision

A campus where no item stays lost for long, and the spirit of “Laus Deo Semper” shows in how we care for one another.
